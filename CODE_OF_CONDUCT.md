# Blop Browser Code of Conduct

The Blop Browser community is a technical collaboration space. We expect
contributors, maintainers, and users to make it safe for people to discuss
browser infrastructure, agent safety, tests, and product tradeoffs without
harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

Community participants are expected to act professionally and constructively.

- Give specific technical feedback and explain its impact.
- Assume mistakes can be corrected, while still treating security boundaries
  and user data with appropriate care.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions focused on the project.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and compatibility without
  personal attacks.
- Remove credentials, personal data, private screenshots, and authenticated
  browser state from examples and reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior isn't acceptable in project spaces or when representing
the project publicly:

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private
  information without permission.
- Pressuring someone to reveal credentials, browser data, identity, or private
  communications.
- Using project infrastructure to target people or services without
  authorization.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and project events. It also applies when someone officially
represents Blop Browser or the `blop-oss` organization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject changes,
issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They will
consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and whether the participant
took responsibility for correcting the behavior.

The project does not publish a verified private conduct email. For conduct on
GitHub, use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
instead of opening a public issue. Include links, dates, and a concise account
of the behavior. Remove credentials and unrelated personal data. Product
vulnerabilities use the `[Security]` process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); suspected misuse of Blop Browser uses the
`[Abuse]` process in [`ACCEPTABLE_USE.md`](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).

GitHub handles reports submitted through its form under its platform policies.
Project maintainers may also act on conduct they can observe in project spaces.
They will not knowingly assign review to a maintainer named in the complaint.
